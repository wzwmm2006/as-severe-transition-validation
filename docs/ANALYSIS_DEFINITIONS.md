# Analysis definitions

## Qualifying moderate AS

The first adult TTE with AVA >1.0 and ≤1.5 cm² and LVEF <50% qualified. If reported, peak velocity had to be <4.0 m/s and mean gradient <40 mmHg. Missing velocity or gradient values were unavailable.

## Native-valve population

Patients with a documented SAVR or TAVR before the qualifying examination, or between that examination and the first severe-range transition, were excluded. A procedure on the same calendar date was retained because procedure time was unavailable.

## First severe-range transition

The first later TTE with AVA ≤1.0 cm², peak velocity ≥4.0 m/s, or mean gradient ≥40 mmHg was selected. Later examinations were not selected according to severity.

## Same-study concordance

At least two severe-range criteria had to be present, including peak velocity or mean gradient.

## Confirmation opportunity and confirmation

The first evaluable TTE 30–365 days after the transition was used. Evaluable meant at least one reported AVA, peak velocity, or mean gradient. Confirmation required at least one observed parameter to remain severe.

## Parameter persistence and schema

Parameter persistence required the same parameter at index and follow-up. Schema A and B are structured-report formats and are not interpreted as equipment eras or accuracy classes.

## Supportive outcomes

Mortality begins at the confirmatory TTE and is administratively limited by the analytical horizon and source ascertainment. Kaplan–Meier and Efron-tie Cox methods are used. AVR/TAVR cumulative incidence uses Aalen–Johansen estimation with death as a competing event. These outcomes provide context and do not redefine the primary echocardiographic estimand.
